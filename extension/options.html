    What the agent does here

    • Opens only the sites above, over https, in its own tab. It never reads or clicks in your other tabs.
    • Asks you first before a click that would buy, subscribe, post, sign in or delete, in every mode.
    • Works in your profile, signed in as you: on the allowed sites it reads and clicks as you would.
    • Cancels downloads its pages start, and leaves CAPTCHAs to you.
    • Makes no network requests of its own: it talks only to debbie, on this computer.
